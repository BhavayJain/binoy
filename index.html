About the doctor

A steady, familiar hand — from first breath to first day of school and beyond.

Practising in Nalbari for over two decades, Dr. Binoy Sharma has cared for generations of children across Assam. She combines the rigour of modern pediatric medicine with the warmth families remember long after the visit is over.

Gentle, family-centred

Every consultation is unhurried. We explain, listen, and treat the child — not just the symptoms.

Newborn to teens

Growth checks, immunisations, common illnesses, developmental care, and adolescent health — under one roof.

Trusted credentials

MBBS from Gauhati Medical College & Hospital and DCH-qualified, with active Assam Medical Council registration.
